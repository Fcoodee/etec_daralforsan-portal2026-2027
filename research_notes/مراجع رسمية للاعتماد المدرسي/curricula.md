## Q1. Which official pages define the national curriculum requirements that foreign/international schools must also deliver?

### Takeaway
Two verified primary documents matter: the Foreign Schools Regulation (Article 13: Arabic/Islamic civilization + Kingdom history/geography, min. 1 hour/week) and the Curriculum Development Center's "Guide to Developed Study Plans, 2nd ed. (1444H)", which contains a separate "national identity subjects" plan for international/private schools following international programmes. The new National Curriculum Center (independent since Feb 2024) is meant to approve a national curriculum framework; its own site could not be opened.

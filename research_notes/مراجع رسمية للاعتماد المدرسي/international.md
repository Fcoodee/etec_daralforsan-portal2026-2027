# International accreditation bodies and partnership frameworks for foreign/international-programme schools in KSA (American-diploma focus)

Verification date: 22 Nov 2026. Method: web search + page fetch. Several official URLs (aiaasc.org, the ETEC list PDF, the MoE accreditation-procedures PDF, sabq, arabnews) could not be fetched in this environment (allowlist/client errors) and are marked as such. Nothing below is inferred recognition.

## 1. What is AIAAsc: official site, standards, and Saudi recognition status

### Takeaway
AIAAsc (American International Accreditation Association of Schools and Colleges) is a real US-registered accreditor that partnered with WASC, but I found NO official Saudi (MoE/ETEC) document recognising it. Its Saudi recognition is UNVERIFIED. I could not open its own website, so its standards and process are also unverified from primary sources.

### Cited Findings
- AIAAsc exists as a named US entity with registered trademarks (the owner name is "American International Accreditation Association of Schools and Colleges"); this is a trademark registry, not evidence of recognition — [Justia trademarks](https://trademark.justia.com/owners/american-international-accreditation-association-of-schools-and-colleges-4145941)
- ACS WASC (a recognised US accreditor) published an announcement of a partnership with AIAASC "to offer joint accreditation to international schools worldwide". It says both bodies have "carefully structured parameters with the high standards and indicators". Quoted AIAASC officials: chairman Dr Ronald J. Kovach; associate executive director Dr Mohanalakshmi. WASC accreditation is described as a route to AIAASC membership. The page is undated (footer shows 2026 copyright only), gives no AIAASC URL, no specific standards and no Saudi mention — [ACS WASC announcement](https://acswasc.org/wasc-partners-with-aiaasc-to-offer-joint-accreditation-to-international-schools/)
- AIRC (American International Recruitment Council) announced a partnership with AIAASC in Feb 2023 (title only seen in search results; page not fetched; relevance to schools in Saudi is none shown) — [AIRC news](https://airc-education.org/news-and-blog/american-international-recruitment-council-and-american-international-accreditation-association-of-schools-and-colleges-announce-a-new-partnership-february-2023)
- A Saudi-school page (Dar Jana International Schools) claims "Cognia accredited as American Diploma schools" and does not mention AIAAsc; it is a school's own claim, not official recognition — [DJIS accreditations](https://djis.edu.sa/dar-jana-accreditations)
- Searches for "aiaasc.org", for AIAAsc + Saudi MoE/ETEC, and for AIAAsc + Jeddah returned no MoE/ETEC page, press release or list naming AIAAsc.

### Inferences
- Absence of AIAAsc from any Saudi official source I could reach means the school's "accredited by AIAAsc" statement should be treated as a private-accreditor claim; it is not shown to satisfy ETEC's "international body accredited by the Commission" route (see Q3).
- The WASC partnership is positive third-party evidence of legitimacy in the US/international market, but is not Saudi recognition.

### Gaps
- AIAAsc's official website URL, standards document, and accreditation process: not retrieved (aiaasc.org not fetchable here). Must be checked directly by the team.
- Whether AIAAsc is licensed or registered by ETEC: no evidence found. Ask ETEC (see email in Q2) or check ETEC's published list of licensed foreign accreditors, which I could not locate.
- Whether AIAAsc is recognised by the US Department of Education/CHEA/CIS: not verified (it did not surface in the CHEA results I saw).

## 2. Other international accreditors active with Saudi schools, and MoE/ETEC pages on international schools, publishers and equivalency

### Takeaway
ETEC officially opened school accreditation in KSA to licensed foreign accreditors (2022) and has licensed at least MSA-CESS (Middle States, self-announced). I found no official ETEC/MoE list naming Cognia, NEASC/CIS or others. MoE's licensing page for international publishers names ETEC's list of accredited curriculum companies, but the list PDF could not be opened.

### Cited Findings
- MoE page "Reg-priv-inte-sch": international publishers "exempt from additional requirements" must show inclusion on the ETEC list of accredited companies and obtain a content licence from the Curriculum Development Center; curricula from companies not on the ETEC list go through the standard accreditation procedures; requests to be added go to Curriculum.Pub.List@etec.gov.sa — [MoE (EN)](https://moe.gov.sa/en/education/general-education/reg-priv-inte-sch) ; also [older URL](https://www.moe.gov.sa/en/education/generaleducation/pages/reg-priv-inte-sch.aspx)
- Linked PDFs (ETEC accredited-companies list; accreditation requirements) exist on moe.gov.sa but were NOT fetchable here: [ETEC list PDF](https://moe.gov.sa/media/uploads/41/41348e85b567af60e21c6ec35c0524755d24e116b527029047fd0f522f63baaf.pdf), [procedures PDF](https://moe.gov.sa/media/uploads/6d/6dedfa3861e71ea709373b3941d79db49906c2b24ae5842ac120d64c2b06c0cb.pdf). The ETEC list is therefore a list of curriculum publishers/companies, NOT of school accreditors.
- ETEC (Tamayuz Center) announced (Ajel, 29 Sep 2022) that school evaluation/accreditation activity is opened to international accreditation bodies, held a session "تنظيم عمل جهات الاعتماد الأجنبية في المملكة" with several international accreditors plus MoE and Ministry of Investment representatives; no accreditors named. Press source, not ETEC itself — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/ki26sahca4)
- MSA-CESS (Middle States) states it received a licence from ETEC (posted 27 Jan 2025). Self-announcement; no ETEC page confirming — [MSA-CESS](https://msa-cess.org/38428-2/)
- ETEC (via the national centre Tamayouz) accredits schools through self-evaluation then external evaluation; it works with MoE; covers public, private and international sectors (SPA report, 19 Mar 2023) — [Saudi Gazette](https://www.saudigazette.com.sa/article/630833)
- ETEC signed national school accreditation agreement with Misk Schools (dated 5/12/2022, format ambiguous); page does not mention international accreditors — [Misk Schools](https://www.miskschools.edu.sa/news/etec-and-misk-schools-sign-agreement-to-implement-school-accreditation)
- A Sabq headline says ETEC signed national school accreditation agreements with 8 educational companies covering 39 schools (headline seen in search only; page not fetchable; names/date unverified) — [Sabq](https://sabq.org/article/5gtsyrA)
- MoE equivalency page: for certificates from non-Saudi systems the school "must be accredited by the authority responsible for education in that country"; the secondary certificate must be recognised by universities in the issuing country; documents attested by issuing education authority, foreign ministry and Saudi embassy; processed via regional education department/Aamali. Does not mention American diplomas, AIAAsc or other accreditors — [MoE equivalency](https://moe.gov.sa/en/reference-and-support/equivalency-and-accreditation/educational-certificates-equivalency)
- MoE Foreign (International) Schools Regulation (Council of Ministers decision no. 26, 1418H): MoE licenses and supervises foreign schools (Art. 3); licence by Minister on supervisory-board proposal (Art. 6); board approves programmes and curricula (Art. 9); one hour/week Arabic, Islamic culture, Kingdom history/geography (Art. 13); annual budget and report (Arts. 14-15). No mention of accreditation or ETEC — [MoE regulation PDF](https://moe.gov.sa/en/aboutus/nationaltransformation/RPRLibrary/International%20Schools%20Regulations.pdf); Arabic version [لائحة المدارس الأجنبية](https://moe.gov.sa/ar/aboutus/aboutministry/RPRLibrary/%D9%85%D8%B1%D9%81%D9%82%202%20%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%8A%D9%94%D8%AD%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%B3%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%94%D8%AC%D9%86%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9.pdf)
- Third-party list of accreditor types (CIS, Cognia, WASC, MSA, NEASC, etc.) from an international school; no Saudi content, not official — [ISS](https://www.iss.edu/wp-content/uploads/Accreditation-Agencies.pdf)
- Cognia, NEASC, College Board/AP, Cambridge, IB, Pearson: I found NO MoE/ETEC page or Saudi-government statement recognising any of them. Only school/portal claims (e.g. DJIS above; mdares.ai is a commercial directory whose pages do not claim official recognition). IB: IBO lists an authorised IB school in Jeddah (American International School of Jeddah) — [IBO](https://ibo.org/school/062223/) — this shows IBO authorisation of a school, not Saudi state recognition.

### Inferences
- ETEC has a licensing regime for foreign accreditors, so a school's international accreditor should be checked against that licensing, not assumed recognised.
- The "exempt publisher" route on the MoE page concerns curriculum publishers; it does not make a school's accreditor (AIAAsc) recognised.

### Gaps
- ETEC's public list of licensed foreign accreditors: not found. Likely ETEC portal (etec.gov.sa); not reachable via search/fetch here.
- Content of the ETEC accredited curriculum-companies PDF: not readable here.
- Official Saudi recognition of Cognia, NEASC/CIS, Middle States (other than MSA-CESS self-claim), College Board, Cambridge, Pearson: not found.
- Official MoE page for the "Management of Foreign and International Programs": not found.

## 3. What MoE/ETEC officially say about aligning international accreditation with national requirements

### Takeaway
The new General Education Law (published 24 Jul 2026, in force 20 Jan 2027) makes accreditation mandatory for private schools, from ETEC or from an international body accredited by ETEC; which bodies qualify is not yet confirmed. International programmes require MoE approval. The primary law text was not retrieved; findings come from law-firm briefings.

### Cited Findings
- Law published in the official gazette 24 Jul 2026 (approved by Council of Ministers 7 Jul 2026); in force 20 Jan 2027; replacement rules due by 20 Jan 2028; implementing regulations date TBC. Accreditation is a legal requirement for every private school, from ETEC or an international body ETEC approves; which bodies ETEC will recognise "still to be confirmed"; international programmes require Ministry approval; national identity subjects mandatory; Arabic primary language — [Al Tamimi & Co (law firm)](https://www.tamimi.com/news/opening-a-school-in-saudi-arabia-what-the-new-education-law-means-for-international-educators/) and [PDF](https://www.tamimi.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Opening-a-School-in-Saudi-Arabia-What-the-New-Education-Law-Means-for-International-Educators-September-2026.pdf)
- Clifford Chance briefing: private institutions must obtain accreditation "from the Education and Training Evaluation Commission" or "from one of the international bodies accredited by the Commission"; the Law covers "approving international programmes in private educational institutions" — [Clifford Chance](https://www.cliffordchance.com/content/dam/cliffordchance/briefings/2026/10/new-education-law-key-developments.pdf) (briefing wording, not the statute)
- Related ETEC practice for training institutions (not schools): foreign accreditation body must be licensed by ETEC; institutions register the accreditation decision with ETEC — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2218911) (press, undated, different sector; shown only as a pattern)

### Inferences
- Under the new Law, Dar Al-Forsan's accreditor will likely need to be one ETEC has accredited/licensed, or the school will need ETEC national accreditation; AIAAsc's status on that point is unverified.
- ETEC national accreditation (the portal's focus) is the safe path regardless of international accreditor.

### Gaps
- Primary law text (Umm Al-Qura gazette / Bureau of Experts) not retrieved; article numbers unknown.
- No official MoE/ETEC document found mapping international accreditation standards to ETEC's domains/indicators (equivalency or alignment).
- Official MoE/ETEC statements about American-diploma schools specifically: none found.

## How does ETEC treat private/foreign/international-programme schools in accreditation?

### Takeaway
Per ETEC's own platform, accreditation is optional and open to private and international schools; it has three stages and yields 5-year full or 2-year conditional accreditation. ETEC e-services list separate national and international accreditation requests for private/international schools.

### Cited Findings
- School accreditation is official recognition from ETEC (or a licensed body) that a school meets conditions and standards for a set period; optional; open to private and international schools; three stages (preparation and nomination, self and external evaluation, final decision); outcome: full accreditation for five years or conditional accreditation for two years — [se.etec.gov.sa](https://se.etec.gov.sa/)
- E-services for institutions include "National School Accreditation Request" and "Request approval for international school accreditation", both for private and international schools in the Kingdom (principals submit) — [E-services catalogue: institutions](https://etec.gov.sa/en/eservices-catalogue/institutions). VERIFIED. Priority: HIGH.
- Nafes results are used to evaluate public schools, accredit private and international schools, and rate general-education schools — [Nafes page (AR)](https://etec.gov.sa/ar/programs/nafs)

### Inferences
- For a private/international school like Dar Al-Forsan, the relevant path is the national accreditation request plus (optionally) the international accreditation approval request.

### Gaps
- No official document found detailing how international-programme accreditation is aligned/recognized against national standards; no verified policy PDF.

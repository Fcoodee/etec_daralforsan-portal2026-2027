## Q4. Does MoE link to or reference ETEC standards or school accreditation?

### Takeaway
Yes, but narrowly: the private/international schools page references ETEC's list of accredited international curriculum publishers. MoE also has a page topic "التقويم والتصنيف والاعتماد المدرسي" but no ETEC reference was visible on it.

### Cited Findings
- International publishers can be licensed without additional requirements if on the ETEC-accredited companies list and holding a Curriculum Development Center content licence; others must go through applicable accreditation procedures; list-inclusion requests go to Curriculum.Pub.List@etec.gov.sa — [MoE EN page](https://www.moe.gov.sa/en/education/generaleducation/Pages/Reg-priv-inte-sch.aspx)
- Page "التقويم والتصنيف والاعتماد المدرسي / School Evaluation, Classification and Accreditation" (under Reference and Support) describes school evaluation as a diagnostic process; ETEC not mentioned; content truncated — https://www.moe.gov.sa/en/reference-and-support/equivalency-and-accreditation — VERIFIED (partial). Priority High.
- my.gov.sa mentions the Education Evaluation Commission only for aptitude exam registration — [my.gov.sa](https://my.gov.sa/en/content/EducationInKSA)

### Inferences
- The MoE accreditation page appears to be the MoE-side anchor for school evaluation; the sub-page behind the heading should be opened to see whether it links to ETEC standards.

### Gaps
- Not covered or not found: unified national curriculum framework pages; MoE school-quality/performance initiatives; MoE moe.gov.sa main portal root (fetch blocked by allowlist, but https://www.moe.gov.sa/ar appeared in search results, SEARCH-ONLY); Unified Document Library. 
- Several moe.gov.sa pages fail intermittently; retry before relying on UNVERIFIED items.
